Cash Cape: Money Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Package name: com.cashcape.app

Cash Cape: Money Tracker (“Cash Cape”, “the app”, package name com.cashcape.app) is a personal finance tracker that works entirely on your phone. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Cash Cape has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere. We (the developer) never receive, see, or store any of your information.

Data stored on your phone

Everything you enter (accounts, transactions, debts and loans, EMI schedules, budgets, goals, recurring items, your name and settings) is stored only on your device.

  • Encrypted database: your financial data is kept in an encrypted database. The encryption key is generated on your phone and protected by the Android Keystore. It never leaves your device.
  • App lock (optional): if you turn on app lock, your PIN is stored only as a salted, one-way hash, never as the PIN itself. Failed PIN attempts are counted on your phone to slow down guessing. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled entirely by Android; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Screen privacy: while app lock is on, the app hides its contents from screenshots and the recent-apps preview.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your data is not copied to Google or to another phone automatically.

Backups and exports

You decide when to create a backup or export, and where it is saved.

  • Encrypted backup: a backup is a file protected by a password you choose. The password is never stored by the app, and we cannot recover it. If you forget it, the backup cannot be opened.
  • CSV and PDF exports are not encrypted. Anyone who gets the file can read it, so keep them private.
  • Where files go: you pick the location through Android’s file picker. If you choose a cloud service (such as Google Drive) or share a file with another app, that service’s or app’s own privacy policy applies to the file from then on.

We cannot recover your data or your backup password, because we never have access to either. If you uninstall the app or lose your phone without a backup, your data is gone.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you about EMIs, money owed, recurring payments, and budget limits. Optional; you can turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face if you turn this on.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or SMS, and has no internet permission.

Deleting your data

You are in control of your data. To erase it, use Settings > Delete all data, or uninstall the app. Backup and export files you created are separate files; delete them yourself from where you saved them.

Children

Cash Cape is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not designed for young children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, there is no children’s data for us to collect, store, or share. If you are under 18, please use the app with a parent or guardian’s guidance, especially before saving backups or exports to cloud services.

Financial information

Cash Cape is a personal tracking tool. It does not offer loans, handle payments, connect to bank accounts, or give financial, tax, or legal advice. Calculations such as EMI schedules and interest are estimates to help you plan; confirm them with your lender.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date, and the app’s behaviour described here will still match what the app does.
